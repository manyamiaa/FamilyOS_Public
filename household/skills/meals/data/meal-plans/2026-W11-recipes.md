## Lentil and roasted veg grain bowl
Lunch Mon–Wed. Batch-cook 6 servings on Sunday.
- 1½ cups green or brown lentils
- 1½ cups quinoa
- 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
- 1 head broccoli, cut into florets
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 5 oz baby spinach
- For the dressing: ⅓ cup tahini, juice of 2 lemons, 1 garlic clove (grated), salt, water to thin

1. Roast the sweet potato, broccoli and onion with the olive oil, cumin, salt and pepper at 425°F for 25–30 minutes.
2. Simmer the lentils in salted water until just tender, 20–25 minutes. Cook the quinoa.
3. Whisk the dressing. Pack each bowl with spinach, quinoa, lentils and veg; keep the dressing separate.

## Chicken shawarma bowl
Lunch Thu–Fri. Batch-cook 4 servings on Wednesday evening.
- 1½ lb boneless chicken thighs
- 1 cup plain yogurt (half for the marinade, half for the sauce)
- 2 tsp shawarma spice blend
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup basmati rice
- 2 cucumbers, diced
- 2 tomatoes, diced

1. Marinate the chicken in half the yogurt, the spice blend, 1 garlic clove, half the lemon juice and the oil for at least 1 hour.
2. Roast at 425°F for 20–25 minutes, rest, then slice.
3. Cook the rice. Stir the remaining garlic, lemon and a pinch of salt into the rest of the yogurt.
4. Pack rice, chicken, cucumber and tomato with the yogurt sauce on the side.

## Chicken karahi, kachumber, roti
- 1 lb bone-in chicken pieces
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 3 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 green chilies, slit
- 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp red chili powder, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp garam masala
- Cilantro, to finish
- For the kachumber: 1 cucumber, 1 tomato, ½ red onion (all finely diced), juice of 1 lemon, salt
- 4 roti

1. Brown the chicken in the oil over high heat. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook 1 minute.
2. Add the tomatoes, spices and salt. Cook covered for 15 minutes, then uncovered on high until the oil separates and the chicken is cooked through.
3. Add the chilies and garam masala, finish with cilantro. Toss the kachumber and warm the roti.

## Palak paneer, jeera rice
- 8 oz paneer, cubed
- 10 oz spinach
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 green chili
- ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp garam masala
- ¼ cup heavy cream
- For the rice: 1 cup basmati rice, 1 tbsp butter, 1 tsp cumin seeds

1. Blanch the spinach for 1 minute, cool, and blend with the green chili.
2. Cook the onion until golden, add the ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes and spices, and cook down.
3. Stir in the spinach and cream, simmer 5 minutes, add the paneer.
4. For the rice, sizzle the cumin seeds in butter, add the rinsed rice and 1¾ cups water, and cook covered.

## Sheet-pan salmon, potatoes, green beans
Not South Asian; the one break in the week.
- 2 salmon fillets (6 oz each)
- 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
- 8 oz green beans
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 lemon

1. Roast the potatoes with half the oil at 425°F for 15 minutes.
2. Add the green beans, then the salmon rubbed with garlic, paprika, salt and the rest of the oil. Roast 12–15 minutes more.
3. Finish with lemon.

## Masoor dal, bhindi, roti
- 1 cup masoor dal (red lentils), rinsed
- 1 onion and 1 tomato for the dal, chopped
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 green chili
- For the tadka: 1 tbsp ghee, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 2 garlic cloves, sliced
- 12 oz okra, sliced
- 1 onion for the bhindi, sliced
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- ½ tsp red chili powder, 1 tsp amchur
- 4 roti

1. Simmer the dal with 3 cups water, the onion, tomato, turmeric, chili and salt until soft, about 20 minutes.
2. Fry the cumin seeds and garlic in the ghee until golden and pour over the dal.
3. Stir-fry the okra in the oil on high heat until it stops being sticky, add the onion and cook until soft, then the chili powder, amchur and salt.
4. Warm the roti.
