# Chana Masala

> Sample recipe for the public repo. Your own recipes go in this folder, one file each.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2 tomatoes, pureed
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 2 tsp chana masala
- 2 cans (15 oz) chickpeas, drained
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped

## Steps
1. Heat the oil, add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds.
2. Add the onion and cook until deep golden, about 10 minutes. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook 1 minute.
3. Add the tomato puree and spices. Cook until the oil separates, 6–8 minutes.
4. Add the chickpeas, water and salt. Simmer 15 minutes, mashing a few chickpeas to thicken.
5. Finish with cilantro. Serve with rice or roti.
