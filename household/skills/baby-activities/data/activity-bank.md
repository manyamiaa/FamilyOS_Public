## 0–2 months

### High-contrast card gazing  <!-- slug: high-contrast-cards -->
- **Area:** sensory
- **Age window:** 0–2 months
- **Uses:** black-and-white drawings on paper, or the high-contrast board book
- **How:** Hold a bold black-and-white picture about 8–12 inches from his face. Let him look, then slowly move it side to side and see if his eyes follow.
- **Why it helps:** Supports visual focus and tracking.
- **Source:** AAP / HealthyChildren.org, baby vision development (topic by name)
- **Safety:** Keep paper out of reach of his mouth.
