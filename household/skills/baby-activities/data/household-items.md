# Things around the house we can use

Starter list; edit to match what we actually have. Toys and books are tracked separately by the `baby-inventory` skill.
Safety: anything small enough to fit through a toilet-paper tube (~1.75 in / 4.5 cm) is a choking hazard and is only for supervised *looking*, never for handling.

## Household items
- Muslin cloths / soft scarves (peekaboo, texture, gentle swinging)
- Wooden spoons, silicone spatulas
- Metal and plastic mixing bowls, small pots with lids (banging, nesting)
- Plastic water bottles (sealed sensory bottles: glue the lid shut)
- Crinkly paper / clean packing paper
- Unbreakable mirror (or the bathroom/dresser mirror)
- Cardboard boxes, paper-towel tubes
- Black-and-white printouts / high-contrast cards drawn on paper
- Spare socks (rattle socks, foot-finding)
- Rolled towel (tummy-time support)
- Flashlight (shadow play)
- Kitchen whisk, measuring cups
