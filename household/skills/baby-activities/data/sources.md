# Trusted sources for activities and milestones

The baby-activities skill only suggests what these sources support, and cites the specific page.
Add your own with "add <site/book> as a source". Mark favorites with ⭐ to prefer them.

## Default sources
| Source | URL | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| American Academy of Pediatrics, HealthyChildren.org | https://www.healthychildren.org | Development by age, play, safety, sleep, feeding |
| CDC: Learn the Signs. Act Early. | https://www.cdc.gov/ncbddd/actearly/ | Milestone checklists (2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 24, 30, 36 mo) and activities to try at each age |
| Zero to Three | https://www.zerotothree.org | Early learning, play, language and social-emotional development |
| NHS Start for Life | https://www.nhs.uk/start-for-life/ | Play and development ideas by age, tummy time, talking with your baby |
| Harvard Center on the Developing Child | https://developingchild.harvard.edu | "Serve and return" interaction, brain-building activities |
| Raising Children Network (Australia) | https://raisingchildren.net.au | Practical play ideas by age, expert-reviewed |
| Pathways.org | https://pathways.org | Motor milestones, tummy time, physical-therapy-informed activities |
| World Health Organization | https://www.who.int | Physical activity, sedentary and screen-time guidelines for under-5s |
